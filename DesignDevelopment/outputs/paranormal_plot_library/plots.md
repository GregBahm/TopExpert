# Paranormal plot source library

Research pass: October 11, 2026. **134 verified complete plots; 12 source records without a completed plot; 0 incomplete plot records.**

This is a source collection with endings spoiled. Entries compress published stories into a premise and actual final outcome. Complete does not mean a solved supernatural mechanism or a permanently defeated antagonist. SCP incident arcs qualify only when the source supplies a developed event and payoff. Static descriptions are separated below.

The initial four lanes were examples, not scope limits. Source discovery added television anthology and radio lanes and a broader future queue. See [source map](source_map.md) and [coverage and limitations](coverage.md).

## The X-Files

### Squeeze — S01E03

**Premise:** Office workers are murdered in locked rooms, their livers removed by an intruder who can squeeze through tiny openings.

**How it plays out:** The ageless killer eats five livers before hibernating for thirty years; agents catch him attacking Scully. Institutionalized, he studies the food slot with a grin.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squeeze_(The_X-Files))

### Shadows — S01E06

**Premise:** A grieving office worker survives attacks while an invisible force kills her attackers.

**How it plays out:** Her murdered boss's ghost protects her and exposes a hidden disk implicating his business partner in illegal sales. She takes a new job, where further activity suggests the ghost has followed her.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadows_(The_X-Files))

### Ice — S01E08

**Premise:** Investigators stranded at an Arctic research station discover parasites that turn their hosts violently paranoid.

**How it plays out:** They learn that two worms in one host kill each other and use a second worm to cure the infected scientist. The survivors are evacuated, and the government destroys the station.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_(The_X-Files))

### Space — S01E09

**Premise:** A shuttle mission suffers sabotage linked to a former astronaut possessed by an entity he encountered in space.

**How it plays out:** The entity threatens the shuttle's return, but its host warns investigators in time to change the landing trajectory. Still struggling against possession in hospital, he jumps to his death.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_(The_X-Files))

### Eve — S01E11

**Premise:** Two fathers die simultaneously across the country, leaving daughters who look exactly alike.

**How it plays out:** The girls are engineered clones who independently knew to murder their fathers; they kill their creator and nearly poison the agents. Captured and institutionalized, they recognize another clone arriving, suggesting their shared knowledge continues.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eve_(The_X-Files))

### Fire — S01E12

**Premise:** A wealthy family seeks protection from a killer who can ignite fires by thought.

**How it plays out:** The killer infiltrates their household and sets it ablaze, but the children are rescued and fuel thrown at him makes him ignite himself. He survives in custody, heals rapidly, and asks for a cigarette.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_(The_X-Files))

### Beyond the Sea — S01E13

**Premise:** After an agent sees her recently dead father, a condemned killer offers psychic clues to a kidnapping in exchange for clemency.

**How it plays out:** His clues rescue the captives, and a warning keeps her from following their abductor onto a fatal collapsing gantry. He is executed without the agent attending; she declines his promised final message from her father.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyond_the_Sea_(The_X-Files))

### Gender Bender — S01E14

**Premise:** Nightclub patrons die after sex with someone who appears to change sex, leading investigators to a secluded religious community.

**How it plays out:** The community can transform bodies and uses irresistible attraction; its members reclaim the rogue killer before agents can arrest them. Their settlement is abandoned overnight, leaving a crop circle that suggests alien departure.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_Bender_(The_X-Files))

### Lazarus — S01E15

**Premise:** An agent revived after a bank shootout appears to carry the consciousness of the robber who died beside him.

**How it plays out:** He reunites with the robber's partner and takes Scully hostage, but his diabetic host needs insulin. His partner withholds it; he shoots her before dying, and his watch remains stopped at the agent's original death.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarus_(The_X-Files))

### Miracle Man — S01E18

**Premise:** A young faith healer loses confidence when people die during his healing services.

**How it plays out:** A man he once resurrected is poisoning followers out of resentment over his disfigured survival. The healer is beaten to death in jail, confronts the culprit as an apparition, and later disappears from the morgue amid reports of him walking.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miracle_Man_(The_X-Files))

### Shapes — S01E19

**Premise:** A rancher shoots an apparent attacking beast, but its body is human and his scratched son begins behaving strangely.

**How it plays out:** A shapeshifting curse has passed to the son, who kills his father and attacks the investigators. The sheriff shoots him; an elder predicts another outbreak in eight years as a wolf howls.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapes_(The_X-Files))

### Darkness Falls — S01E20

**Premise:** Investigators reach an abandoned logging camp where ancient insects released from a felled tree attack only in darkness.

**How it plays out:** They ration generator light, then attempt an escape that fails when their vehicle is disabled. The insects engulf them, but rescuers save and quarantine the survivors while authorities try to eradicate the swarm with fire and pesticides.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkness_Falls_(The_X-Files))

### Tooms — S01E21

**Premise:** A liver-eating mutant released from custody needs one final victim before another thirty-year hibernation.

**How it plays out:** He frames his pursuer, murders his doctor, and retreats beneath a shopping mall built over his former lair. When he attacks, the agent escapes and activates the escalator, crushing him.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tooms)

### Born Again — S01E22

**Premise:** A little girl is present when former police colleagues die mysteriously, and she seems to recall a murdered detective's life.

**How it plays out:** She is apparently the detective reincarnated and uses telekinesis to avenge his murder. Investigators uncover the officers' theft and betrayal; she spares the final culprit after his wife's plea, and returns to an ordinary childhood as he confesses.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_Again_(The_X-Files))

### Roland — S01E23

**Premise:** Scientists die at a propulsion laboratory where an intellectually disabled janitor suddenly displays advanced mathematical knowledge.

**How it plays out:** His cryogenically preserved twin appears to control him to punish colleagues who stole his research. Agents stop the final killing as sabotage destroys the twin's preserved remains, but the janitor's departing hairstyle hints that the influence persists.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland_(The_X-Files))

### The Host — S02E02

**Premise:** A corpse in the sewers leads investigators to a humanoid fluke parasite attacking sanitation workers.

**How it plays out:** The captured creature escapes and heads through an overflow pipe toward the ocean. An agent cuts it in half with a sewer grate, but its eyes remain open underground, leaving its survival unresolved.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Host_(The_X-Files))

### Aubrey — S02E12

**Premise:** A pregnant detective experiences visions that uncover old FBI bodies while new murders repeat a decades-old killer's signature.

**How it plays out:** She is the killer's granddaughter, apparently reenacting his crimes through inherited memories. After attacking others she kills her grandfather and surrenders, then is confined under suicide watch following an attempt to end her pregnancy.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aubrey_(The_X-Files))

### Die Hand Die Verletzt — S02E14

**Premise:** A teenager's ritual death exposes a high school's secret Satanist faculty and a sinister substitute teacher.

**How it plays out:** The teacher manipulates deaths while punishing the adults' lapsed devotion. When they try to sacrifice the investigating agents, she makes the remaining cultists kill themselves instead, then disappears after leaving a farewell on the chalkboard.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hand_Die_Verletzt)

### Humbug — S02E20

**Premise:** Murders plague a community of retired sideshow performers, suggesting a small creature is roaming at night.

**How it plays out:** A dying man's detachable conjoined twin is killing people while seeking a replacement brother. It escapes after its brother dies and vanishes near a performer who eats anything; his stomachache and final remark imply he ate it.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humbug_(The_X-Files))

### The Calusari — S02E21

**Premise:** A child's apparent accident is followed by deaths around his older brother and his grandmother's protective rituals.

**How it plays out:** The boy's stillborn twin is the malevolent presence, impersonating him and attacking the family. Mystics complete a separation ritual while the spirit threatens Scully and the mother; it disappears, and mother and surviving son reunite.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Calusari)

### Soft Light — S02E23

**Premise:** People vanish into scorch marks around a physicist whose laboratory accident made his shadow lethal.

**How it plays out:** He seeks shelter in diffuse light, but attempts to capture him cause more deaths. Investigators believe he was destroyed in a particle accelerator; the final scene reveals him alive and subjected to government experiments.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_Light_(The_X-Files))

### D.P.O. — S03E03

**Premise:** A young mechanic who survived lightning can control electricity and uses it against people who frustrate him.

**How it plays out:** He murders his friend and threatens his former teacher until another lightning strike kills the pursuing sheriff. Confined in a psychiatric hospital, he retains his power and uses it to change his television channels.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.P.O.)

### Clyde Bruckman's Final Repose — S03E04

**Premise:** An insurance salesman who foresees people's deaths helps investigate a murderer targeting fortune tellers.

**How it plays out:** His vision appears to predict Mulder's murder, but Scully shoots the attacker and the vision proves to be the killer's final thoughts. They find the salesman dead by suicide; Scully holds his hand on his bed, fulfilling another prediction.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clyde_Bruckman%27s_Final_Repose)

### The Walk — S03E07

**Premise:** Military patients and officers suffer deaths blamed on a phantom soldier who prevents one survivor from killing himself.

**How it plays out:** A quadruple amputee is projecting an astral body to punish commanders by murdering their families. As his apparition attacks again, a surviving victim smothers his physical body, ending the attacks; authorities cannot prove the paranormal murders.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walk_(The_X-Files))

### Oubliette — S03E08

**Premise:** A former kidnapping victim develops a psychic link to a girl abducted by the same man, sharing her injuries.

**How it plays out:** The link leads investigators to the girl being drowned. They kill the abductor, but resuscitation initially benefits the older woman instead; the girl finally revives as the older woman dies, apparently sacrificing herself.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oubliette_(The_X-Files))

### Revelations — S03E11

**Premise:** A murderer of fraudulent stigmatics targets a boy whose bleeding wounds and apparent bilocation seem genuine.

**How it plays out:** Scully follows religious clues to a recycling plant where the killer tries to take the boy into a shredder. The boy catches the edge, the killer falls in, and Scully saves him before seeking confession about the miracles she witnessed.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revelations_(The_X-Files))

### Syzygy — S03E13

**Premise:** A town blames teenage deaths on Satanists while two birthday-sharing girls secretly wield destructive powers.

**How it plays out:** A rare planetary alignment amplifies the girls' cosmic energy, and jealousy turns their shared power murderous. Agents confine them together as the police station erupts in chaos; at midnight the alignment's influence ends and their powers stop.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syzygy_(The_X-Files))

### Grotesque — S03E14

**Premise:** An artist arrested for mutilation murders claims a gargoyle possessed him, and killings continue while he is imprisoned.

**How it plays out:** The investigator obsessed with understanding the artist has become the new killer. He is shot and arrested, yet Mulder's glimpse of him as a gargoyle and a blood-drawn image in his cell leave possession versus madness ambiguous.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grotesque_(The_X-Files))

### Pusher — S03E17

**Premise:** A contract killer can talk people into obeying commands, disguising murders as suicides.

**How it plays out:** His brain tumor may cause the power, which he refused to lose through surgery. He forces Mulder into Russian roulette, but Scully's fire alarm breaks his concentration; Mulder shoots him, leaving him comatose and expected to die.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pusher_(The_X-Files))

### Quagmire — S03E22

**Premise:** Deaths around a lake famous for a dinosaur-like monster draw investigators as the local frog population declines.

**How it plays out:** A boat attack strands the agents, and further pursuit ends when Mulder shoots an alligator believed responsible for the killings. They depart disappointed, while the real lake monster swims past unseen.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quagmire_(The_X-Files))

## Goosebumps

### Welcome to Dead House — Goosebumps-original-01

**Premise:** A family inherits a house in a strangely deserted town, where their children keep seeing unfamiliar people inside.

**How it plays out:** The townspeople are undead who lure fresh victims to sustain themselves with blood. The children expose them to sunlight and rescue their parents, but another family arrives with an agent who resembles the supposedly destroyed undead ringleader.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis; opened](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_Dead_House)

### Stay Out of the Basement — Goosebumps-original-02

**Premise:** Two children investigate their botanist father's locked basement as he develops disturbing plantlike habits.

**How it plays out:** They discover a plant duplicate has imprisoned their real father, identify him by his red blood, and help him destroy the clone. After he clears out his experiments, a surviving flower claims to be their real father.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis; cached excerpt (resolution and twist retrieved); direct open blocked](https://goosebumps.fandom.com/wiki/Stay_Out_of_the_Basement); [secondary review; opened supporting page](https://greatbooksguy.com/2025/06/17/goosebumps-review-stay-out-of-the-basement-1992-by-r-l-stine/)

### Monster Blood — Goosebumps-original-03

**Premise:** A boy visiting his aunt buys novelty slime that keeps growing and makes his dog enormous.

**How it plays out:** The slime begins swallowing people, and the aunt's cat is revealed as a witch controlling it. The giant dog knocks her into the slime, breaking the spell and releasing its victims; the leftover slime then disappears.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis; opened](https://goosebumps.fandom.com/wiki/Monster_Blood_%28book%29)

### Say Cheese and Die! — Goosebumps-original-04

**Premise:** Friends steal a camera whose photographs show disasters that subsequently happen to their subjects.

**How it plays out:** They discover its inventor cursed it after his partner stole it; the thief tries to imprison them to protect the secret. Photographing him kills him, and they hide the camera again, but two bullies retrieve it and photograph themselves.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis; opened](https://goosebumps.fandom.com/wiki/Say_Cheese_and_Die%21)

### The Curse of the Mummy's Tomb — Goosebumps-original-05

**Premise:** A boy visiting an archaeological dig carries a mummy's hand as a charm while mysterious dangers surround the excavation.

**How it plays out:** A guardian of the tomb traps him, his cousin, and his uncle and tries to kill them for disturbing it. The charm animates the mummies, who drive the guardian away, allowing the family to escape; a final apparent mummy scare is only the boy's returning parents.

**Sources:** [detailed synopsis; cached excerpt (resolution and twist retrieved); direct open blocked](https://goosebumps.fandom.com/wiki/The_Curse_of_the_Mummy%27s_Tomb); [secondary review; opened supporting page](https://greatbooksguy.com/2025/09/19/goosebumps-review-5-the-curse-of-the-mummys-tomb-1993-by-r-l-stine/)

**Premise:** A plague-doctor figure kills people by touch and turns their bodies into mindless surgical creations while claiming to cure disease.

**How it plays out:** After months of cooperation it kills and remakes its regular interviewer; its privileges are revoked, but the final interview shows it still insisting the death advanced its cure and requesting living patients.

**Sources:** [full text (primary SCP Wiki article, including embedded addenda)](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-049)

### Time-Traveling Train — SCP-052

**Premise:** A scrapped subway train reappears every Saturday, dropping passengers into different years.

**How it plays out:** Investigators send agents aboard; one returns without hands and carrying a warning to send nobody else, and a later agent’s future corpse is thrown from the train, leaving further boarding tests prohibited.

**Sources:** [full text (primary SCP Wiki article, including embedded addenda)](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-052)

## Attributed folklore

### Two Spirits — FOLK-Wolf-1845-136

**Premise:** A man dresses as a ghost to frighten his wife in a graveyard.

**How it plays out:** A real shrouded skeleton appears beside him; he flees, but the women lock him out and he collapses unconscious until morning.

**Sources:** [full text; attributed published retelling in scholarly edited collection](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/type1676.html) — Two Spirits — Belgium

**Published version:** Johannes Wilhelm Wolf, Deutsche Märchen und Sagen (1845), no. 136, pp. 241–42; oral source.

### The Death Shroud — FOLK-Haase-1887-49

**Premise:** A servant steals a wandering dead man's shroud and hides inside a church.

**How it plays out:** The corpse persuades him to pass a corner through the keyhole and bursts the door open; the servant is found nearly dead and dies young afterward.

**Sources:** [full text; attributed published retelling in scholarly edited collection](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/type1676.html) — The Death Shroud — Germany

**Published version:** Karl Eduard Haase, Volkstümliches aus der Grafschaft Ruppin und Umgegend, vol. 1 (1887), no. 49, p. 53; communicated by Teacher Fehse.

### The Preacher and the Ghost — FOLK-Afzelius-1842-245

**Premise:** A wife sends a sheet-covered servant to frighten her husband returning from midnight prayers.

**How it plays out:** When the preacher challenges him, the servant starts sinking into the earth; his pleas cannot save him, and he disappears completely beneath it.

**Sources:** [full text; attributed published retelling in scholarly edited collection](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/type1676.html) — The Preacher and the Ghost — Sweden

**Published version:** Arvid August Afzelius, Volkssagen und Volkslieder aus Schwedens älterer und neuerer Zeit, vol. 3 (1842), p. 245.

### White Cap — FOLK-Arnason-1864-157

**Premise:** A girl mistakes a graveyard ghost for a familiar prankster and steals its cap.

**How it plays out:** Ordered to replace it silently, she instead speaks; the ghost strikes her dead and sinks back into its grave.

**Sources:** [full text; attributed published retelling in scholarly edited collection](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/type0366.html) — White Cap — Iceland

**Published version:** Jón Arnason, Icelandic Legends, translated by George E. J. Powell and Eiríkur Magnússon (1864), pp. 157–58.

### The Stolen Liver — FOLK-Knoop-1909-85

**Premise:** A husband spends the food money drinking, then substitutes a hanged man's liver for his wife's supper.

**How it plays out:** The dead man arrives demanding it; when she questions him about his missing liver, he seizes and strangles her.

**Sources:** [full text; attributed published retelling in scholarly edited collection](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/type0366.html) — The Stolen Liver — Poland

**Published version:** Otto Knoop, Ostmärkische Sagen, Märchen und Erzählungen (1909), no. 85, pp. 181–82.

### A Bad Composition — SCP-012

**Status:** no_ending. Describes recurring compulsion and deaths across anonymous tests, without a bounded developed narrative.

**Premise:** Archaeologists uncover an unfinished musical score written in human blood.

**How it plays out:** Readers compulsively cut themselves to supply more blood; those who finish a section kill themselves after declaring the composition impossible to complete, and performances produce only discord.

**Sources:** [full text (primary SCP Wiki article, including embedded addenda)](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-012)

### The Morgue — SCP-022

**Status:** no_ending. Morgue mechanics and explanatory testing reveal are verified, but no sufficiently developed central incident concludes in this record.

**Premise:** A hospital morgue begins producing animated corpses that try to leave.

**How it plays out:** The bodies match cadavers missing elsewhere; experiments reveal that objects sent inside emerge upstairs aged and ruined, while entering humans are absorbed into the morgue and can return as its corpses.

**Sources:** [full text (primary SCP Wiki article, including embedded addenda)](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-022)

**Sources:** [full text (primary SCP Wiki article, including embedded addenda)](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-097)
