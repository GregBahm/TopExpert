# Three new Top Expert cases
Supervised researcher-to-designer experiment, October 10, 2026.

Design assumptions: all difficulty, resource amounts, deadlines and checks remain proposals for balancing. Named cards are case-specific suggestions using existing inventory mechanics. Each opportunity consumes investigator time unless described as an item slot. HQ offers reassurance but never rescue: the original organization is destroyed. No engine work or automation was performed.

## 1. Spin Cycle Liability
A laundromat promises to take years off clothes. Ancient trousers emerge pristine while the pavement acquires decades of potholes. The owner wants certification before a promotional weekend. The agency's textile department is Langerak and a tire-pressure warning.

Clues: deterioration follows completed washes. Fresh fabric causes little damage; worn fabric corrodes nearby plumbing. Matched marked samples demonstrate that improving one transfers wear elsewhere. Otherness reveals wear flowing through the drain toward things currently performing useful work. Reveal: the machine reallocates accumulated wear, eventually including working knees, lungs and hearts. A replacement selector was sold as remaining-life recovery; its warranty excludes consequential aging.

Opportunities:
- Interview customers (Interpersonal): Wash History insight. Failure brings compensation demands and obstructed access.
- Controlled loads (Focus): Matched Fabric Samples insight; money buys expendable test supplies. Failure ages equipment or injures the operator.
- Trace service records (Knowledge): corroborates replacement component and exposure timing.
- Inspect drain while running (Otherness + Resilience): locate transfer route; failure exposes investigator to accelerated wear.
- Divert discharge (Sly), aided by evidence: redirect toward a deliberately supplied sacrificial object; failure sends it toward occupied equipment.
- Interrupt dangerous cycle (Violence): immediate rescue, but broken casing leaves existing discharge uncontrolled.

Costs/escalation: business transfers more wear; closing costs income and needs Interpersonal cooperation or compensation. Exploitability may secure unpaid observation, never protection. No numerical timer is prescribed.

Resolution A: finish current discharge into expendable equipment, then disconnect replacement component (Focus with evidence). Neighborhood repair bill remains. Resolution B: retain controlled use with sacrificial materials and recurring maintenance; useful and ethically ugly. Optional follow-up: a customer brings a centuries-old heirloom.

Handoff: routine workplace exposure (Darkness Falls) + beneficial convenience hiding loss (Haunted Mask) + comparative tests/maintenance (SCP-871). Designer replaced source creatures, possession and cakes with transferred wear.
Seed distinction: nearest #15 erratic electronics; these machines work consistently and successfully. New premise is laundry pickup and displaced repair costs, not random device malfunction.

## 2. Please Be Seated
At a backyard wedding, the seating chart enforces etiquette physically. Guests at the children's table shrink when interrupted. A cousin at the head table orders people uphill across level grass. The caterer asks whether supernatural corrections count as additional covers.

Clues: effects start after guests accept assignments. Effects follow acknowledged importance, not chairs or distance. A discreet swap transfers effects with the accepted assignment; unused seats have no power. Knowledge traces secondhand place-card stock to ceremonial rank assignment, corroborating observation. Reveal: the chart turns collectively accepted hierarchy into physical force. Deference and speeches strengthen it.

Opportunities:
- Private interviews (Interpersonal): Family Fault Lines insight; failure draws investigators into arguments.
- Watch successive courses (Focus): connect acknowledgment and effects; waiting improves evidence while guests remain exposed.
- Seating swap (Sly): controlled test; discovery loses trust and can designate investigator as outsider.
- Research stationery (Knowledge): contextual corroboration, not a ready-made solution manual.
- Read room literally (Otherness): sense pressure between positions and identify human anchors.
- Move endangered guests (Resilience); force an escape path (Violence): rescues carry injury/witness costs.

Costs/escalation: dinner speeches consolidate rank until the family head can prevent departure. Ending dinner costs catering money and goodwill; continuing risks bodies. Investigators keep offering refreshments while awaiting the nonexistent agency negotiator.

Resolution A: Interpersonal with Family Fault Lines persuades guests to reject assigned ranks and publicly seat themselves. Equal wording fails if deference persists. Resolution B: Sly induces dominant relatives to accept ceremonial duties requiring release of others; dismantle arrangement after anchors leave. Until then those relatives remain dangerously empowered. Follow-up: a guest requests a leadership retreat.

Handoff: empowering convenience becomes coercive (Haunted Mask) + ordinary activity activates danger (Darkness Falls). Designer changes exposure to accepted social rank and intervention to collective behavior.
Seed distinction: nearest #28 unstoppable concert/#29 circus. Wedding etiquette and rank cause the effect; no ongoing performance or entertainment is required.

## 3. Everything Marked Later
A couple labels boxes of unfinished projects LATER during a house move. The truck's front advances while its rear remains in the driveway. Investigators are hired as specialist movers; nobody mentions the specialist rate.

Clues: unlabeled boxes move normally. Completed work moves despite the label. Finishing one boxed thank-you note frees the truck briefly; promising to finish the rest anchors it again. Effects worsened when the couple declared everything somebody else's problem. Otherness identifies the particular commitment anchoring the transition. Reveal: an organizing stamp makes postponed obligations literal. A task can cross households only after genuine completion, abandonment, or acknowledged acceptance of responsibility. Claims transmit the effect; boxes do not multiply.

Opportunities:
- Sort load (Focus): Unfinished Task Inventory insight; failure wastes time or strands essential equipment.
- Interview household (Interpersonal): identify concealed promises/conflicting ownership; failure encourages blame and further unresolved responsibility.
- Compare control boxes (Focus; Knowledge can contextualize stamp): distinguish obligations from simple spatial trap.
- Locate blocked transition (Otherness): identify active anchor.
- Retrieve possessions (Resilience): recover useful items at injury risk.
- Expose falsely claimed duty (Sly): demonstrate nobody actually undertook it. A fake recipient merely transfers blockage.
- Finish tasks (Focus), or explain abandonment to affected people (Interpersonal): turns spent clearing individual anchors.

Costs/escalation: new promises spread blockage to helpers' journeys and plans. Extra movers cost money and add no solution. Exploitability can make an investigator accept a miserable list, freeing clients while blocking that investigator's next departure. HQ's relocation team is always en route and does not exist.

Resolution A: complete or explicitly abandon anchoring obligations, including uncomfortable conversations; wasted work and dissatisfied clients remain. Resolution B: investigators knowingly accept selected obligations to free move sooner, carrying continuing case opportunities and personal consequences. Follow-up: an inherited task is reconcile with my brother, who has been missing for years.

Handoff: recurring maintenance and failed destructive shortcuts (SCP-871) + burden-removing convenience with hidden cost (Haunted Mask). Designer replaces food and compulsory consumption with genuine decisions about responsibility.
Seed distinction: nearest #8 stairwell/#39 abandoned building. Occupied home, moving-day commitments and blocked transition; no exploration maze or missing explorers.

## Supervisor review and experiment findings

Different investigator routes and proposed insight cards fit the evolving board; no fixed probabilities, currencies, or new global systems are specified. Evidence supports each rule before resolution. Research claims remain confined to verified source descriptions; case details are original designer inventions.

The actual completed research brief was sent explicitly to the separate designer after inherited history failed to expose it. Designer acknowledged receipt before writing. This demonstrates a successful explicit handoff, and a limitation of relying on inherited messages. All cases share rule-discovery and negotiated/maintenance solutions; this trial does not establish broad tonal or mechanical coverage, playability, balance, or exhaustive originality.
