# Paranormal source discovery map

Checked 2026-10-11. **32 collections across 8 categories.** This is a broad queue of research locations, distinct from actually harvested plot rundowns. Eligible volumes below are estimates, never verified story totals. The discovery agent added no plot entries. Catalog pages establish searchable units; only full text/audio or a detailed ending synopsis can establish a complete plot.

Priority A: immediately strong evidence path; B: promising with individual evidence checks; C: additional access/ending research required; D: access failed. These priorities concern harvestability, not artistic quality.

## Collection rules and gaps

Discoveries do not narrow the original task to a few franchises. Keep expanding across geography, language, era and format. This map is presently English-access-heavy; African, South Asian, Latin American and Indigenous published catalogs need further discovery beyond occasional translated magazine stories. Audio and visual comics are underharvested because complete text can be harder to access. No claim of global completeness is made.

For serialized podcasts, record one complete arc with its start and end sources. Night Vale's official index explicitly groups a Locked Library Mystery arc; episode numbering is not a count of independent completed plots. Magnus statements can have a local outcome while the institute frame remains open: preserve that distinction. Open-ended or ambiguous conclusions must be stated as such, never replaced by invented closure.

Cross-catalog reprints, rebroadcasts and translated compilation overlaps need work-level deduplication. Different published adaptations or attributable folktale retellings can remain distinct records with linked provenance. A generic urban legend theme is not itself a published story unit. Source word limits apply cumulatively when several anthology segments derive from the same page.
